Before you sign up

Is this investment advice?

No. We report what moved and why, with sources. We don't know your situation and never tell anyone to buy or sell. Read the full disclaimer.

How do you check the numbers?

Every price and move is checked against closing market data. Every other figure must trace to a source page we opened, and claims are checked against those sources. Anything that reads like a tip is cut.

Which stocks do you cover?

Big US tech and the most-watched tickers: Nvidia, Apple, Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Tesla and the companies moving around them.

When do the emails arrive?

The free brief lands on Saturday. The members' report lands on Sunday, before Monday's open in New York and London.
